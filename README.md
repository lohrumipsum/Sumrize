# Sumrize
Sumrize captures your meeting notes and discussions and generates summaries and to-dos.

# Privacy Policy
Your privacy is the foundation of this application. This Privacy Policy outlines how Sumrize handles your information.

Sumrize is designed to be a private, on-device tool. We do not collect, store, or transmit any of your personal data or meeting content.  All AI processing, including audio transcription, text recognition (OCR), and summary generation, happens **100% on your device** using Apple's native frameworks. Your audio, notes, and transcriptions are never sent to any external server.  All of your core data—summaries, action items, transcriptions, and note images—is stored exclusively on your device in a private, local database managed by SwiftData.  Temporary audio files are deleted from your device immediately after transcription. All other data can be permanently deleted by you at any time from within the app (e.g., by deleting a meeting or folder).

Sumrize uses Apple's native StoreKit for managing the Pro subscription. We do not use any other third-party services for analytics, advertising, or data processing.
